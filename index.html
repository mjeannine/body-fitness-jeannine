Everything you need to start, in one place

Many people want to get fit but don't know where to begin: what to eat, what to avoid, what to drink, or which exercises are right for them.

Body Fitness answers those questions. We share workout videos, notes and food routines you can follow at home, and we run classes at our gym for people who like training with others or need machines they don't have space for.

We keep the workouts simple, so you can make progress without feeling overwhelmed. We also share a few skincare and wellness tips along the way.

Pick the class that fits your life

Train at home, come to the gym, or mix both. You can switch any time.

  • Free

    Online class

    Follow recorded workouts, notes and meal routines from anywhere. No travel, no equipment needed, no one watching.

  • At our gym

    In-person class

    Train on our machines with a coach and a group. Good if you don't have equipment or space at home, or you like working out with others.

  • Best of both

    Hybrid class

    Train online during the week and join us at the gym when you can. Your routine and progress carry over between both.
