Our equipment

Not everyone has the machines or the space to train at home. Our gym floor has everything you need, and our coaches will show you how to use it safely.

Cardio

Build stamina and burn energy at your own pace. Every machine shows your time, distance and heart rate, so you can see your progress week by week.

  • Treadmills
  • Indoor cycling bikes
  • Ellipticals and cross-trainers

Strength

Shape and tone your muscles with free weights and guided machines. Beginners start light, and a coach helps you add weight when you're ready.

  • Dumbbells for every level
  • Cable and resistance machines
  • Benches and stretching area
